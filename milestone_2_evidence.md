## 1. Summary of Deliverables & Coexistence Invariants Verified

Milestone 2 implements [`TestEndToEndCustomConfigCoexistence_ForwardConnectorPrePipeline`](file:///tmp/prototypes/tab7_custom_config_coexistence/components/google-built-opentelemetry-collector/provider/googlecontrolplaneprovider/coexistence_test.go#L1068-L1218) and [`TestEndToEndCustomConfigCoexistence_LiveGCP`](file:///tmp/prototypes/tab7_custom_config_coexistence/components/google-built-opentelemetry-collector/provider/googlecontrolplaneprovider/coexistence_test.go#L1278-L1432) in [`coexistence_test.go`](file:///tmp/prototypes/tab7_custom_config_coexistence/components/google-built-opentelemetry-collector/provider/googlecontrolplaneprovider/coexistence_test.go), verifying all four coexistence invariants end-to-end across a multi-provider `confmap.Resolver` (`uris: ["googlecontrolplane:file://...?project=dashpole-dev", "file:/tmp/.../custom_config.yaml"]`), real OTel Collector `service.New` pipeline graph execution, `googlepolicyprocessor`, `queuebatchprocessor`, `forward` connector, `otlpexporter`, `googleclientauthextension`, and live Cloud Logging in GCP project `dashpole-dev`:

1. **Invariant 1 (Merge & Deduplication)**:
   - In [`TestEndToEndCustomConfigCoexistence_ForwardConnectorPrePipeline`](file:///tmp/prototypes/tab7_custom_config_coexistence/components/google-built-opentelemetry-collector/provider/googlecontrolplaneprovider/coexistence_test.go#L1159-L1166), `googlecontrolplane:file://...` loads `01-drop-debug.json` (`LogFilterPolicy`) and `02-remote-source.json` (`test_remote_source` $\rightarrow$ `otlp/remote_policy_source`), while `file:/tmp/.../custom_config_forward.yaml` attaches `receivers: [sqlquery/direct_app, forward/sep]` to `service.pipelines.logs/googlecontrolplane`. `confmap.Resolver` merges and deduplicates `logs/googlecontrolplane.receivers` to `[nop/googlecontrolplane, otlp/remote_policy_source, sqlquery/direct_app, forward/sep]` while preserving `processors: [googlepolicy, queue_batch/default_gcp_destination_batch_logs]` and `exporters: [otlp_grpc/default_gcp_destination]`.
   - In [`TestEndToEndCustomConfigCoexistence_LiveGCP`](file:///tmp/prototypes/tab7_custom_config_coexistence/components/google-built-opentelemetry-collector/provider/googlecontrolplaneprovider/coexistence_test.go#L1348-L1364), `logs/googlecontrolplane.receivers` merges to `[nop/googlecontrolplane, sqlquery/sep]` with `otlp_grpc/default_gcp_destination` pointing at live `telemetry.googleapis.com:443`.
2. **Invariant 2 (Single Shared `googlepolicy` & Workload `queue_batch` Processor Instance)**:
   - Wrapping `googlepolicyprocessor.NewFactory()` and `queuebatchprocessor.NewFactory()` with [`wrapCountingProcessorFactory`](file:///tmp/prototypes/tab7_custom_config_coexistence/components/google-built-opentelemetry-collector/provider/googlecontrolplaneprovider/coexistence_test.go#L184-L202) confirms during `service.New` startup that **exactly 1** `googlepolicy` logs processor instance (`googlePolicyInstances.Load() == 1`) and **2** total `queue_batch` logs processor instances (`queueBatchInstances.Load() == 2`: `1` in `logs/googlecontrolplane` + `1` in `logs/default_self_metrics`) are instantiated—even when both a remote `SourcePolicy` receiver (`otlp/remote_policy_source`), a direct local receiver (`sqlquery/direct_app`), and a pre-processed local pipeline (`logs/sep_pre` $\rightarrow$ `forward/sep`) feed `logs/googlecontrolplane`.
3. **Invariant 3 (Receiver-Specific Pre-Processing via `forward` Connector)**:
   - [`TestEndToEndCustomConfigCoexistence_ForwardConnectorPrePipeline`](file:///tmp/prototypes/tab7_custom_config_coexistence/components/google-built-opentelemetry-collector/provider/googlecontrolplaneprovider/coexistence_test.go#L1068-L1218) routes `sqlquery/sep` through `logs/sep_pre` (`processors: [transform/sep_only]`, `exporters: [forward/sep]`) into `logs/googlecontrolplane.receivers: [sqlquery/direct_app, forward/sep]`.
   - Both `sqlquery/sep` and `sqlquery/direct_app` emit 1 `DEBUG` watermark log and 1 `INFO` security-alert log. The single shared `googlepolicy` processor in `logs/googlecontrolplane` drops both `DEBUG` logs (`len(records) == 2`), `bySource["sqlquery/sep"]` carries `sep.preprocessed == "true"`, and `bySource["sqlquery/direct_app"]` is NOT mutated by `transform/sep_only` (`directHasPre == false`).
4. **Invariant 4 (Live `telemetry.googleapis.com:443` Export in `dashpole-dev` + Negative Control)**:
   - [`TestEndToEndCustomConfigCoexistence_LiveGCP`](file:///tmp/prototypes/tab7_custom_config_coexistence/components/google-built-opentelemetry-collector/provider/googlecontrolplaneprovider/coexistence_test.go#L1278-L1432) runs two live collector lifecycles exporting to `telemetry.googleapis.com:443` in `dashpole-dev`:
     - **With `LogFilterPolicy` (`changeRunID = a63c0d08-60e4-4a27-906f-0c8ca0622a48`)**: `sqlquery/sep` emits 1 `DEBUG` log and 1 `INFO` log; `googlepolicy` drops the `DEBUG` log and `otlp_grpc/default_gcp_destination` exports the `INFO` log to `telemetry.googleapis.com:443`. Querying Cloud Logging (`projects/dashpole-dev/logs/otlp`) via `gcloud logging read` confirms `count(INFO) == 1` (`insertId=1t8a1icf3kmm3x`) and `count(DEBUG) == 0`.
     - **Negative Control Without `LogFilterPolicy` (`baselineRunID = 7fc83f3e-64cb-4f2b-933c-28b62f7db54f`)**: With an empty policy directory, both `INFO` (`insertId=1t8a1icf3kmm5e`) and `DEBUG` (`insertId=1t8a1icf3kmm5d`) logs from `sqlquery/sep` arrive in Cloud Logging in `dashpole-dev` (`count(INFO) == 1`, `count(DEBUG) == 1`), proving that the `DEBUG` absence in the `changeRunID` run was caused specifically by `googlepolicy` evaluating `01-drop-debug.json` inside `logs/googlecontrolplane`.
